# About Music

Holiday Lights can play festive background music while you work, while the screen saver is running, or both. Music stays off until you turn it on.

Holiday Lights includes 46 songs: Christmas carols and classics, Chanukah, Halloween and New Year songs, patriotic tunes and folk favorites, arranged by the musicians named in [Music Copyright Information](topic:music-copyright). You can easily add your own songs: MIDI files work, and so do MP3, WAV, WMA, AIFF, AU, MPEG and M4A files.

All music settings are on the **Music Box** page of Holiday Lights Settings:

- **Play Holiday Music** turns all music on or off. See [Turning Music On and Off](topic:music-on-off).
- The song list chooses which songs can play. See [Turning Songs On and Off](topic:songs-on-off).
- **Play the Chosen Songs** chooses when music plays. See [Choosing When Music is Played](topic:when-music-plays).
- The volume slider sets how loud the music is. See [Volume](topic:volume).
- **Make the Lights Dance to the Music** lets your lights play along. See [Making the Lights Dance](topic:lights-dance).

If you don't hear anything, see [Solving Music Problems](topic:music-problems).
