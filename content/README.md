# content - the bulbs, songs and pictures that ship with Holiday Lights

This folder holds the content that Holiday Lights 6 installs next to the program. The build copies it unchanged into
the output folder (`HolidayLights.App.csproj`, item group "Bundled content"), where the app finds it as
`Content\Bulbs`, `Content\Music` and `Content\Pictures`; `tools/publish/publish.ps1` checks the counts.

| Folder | Files | What they are |
|---|---|---|
| `Bulbs/` | 1,501 `.bul` files | The add-on bulb collection of Holiday Lights 5.4: animated holiday bulbs drawn by 379 artists |
| `Music/` | 46 `.mid` files | The holiday songs of the Holiday Lights 5.4 Music Box (Standard MIDI Files) |
| `Pictures/` | 11 `.BMP` files | The background pictures of the Holiday Lights 5.4 screen saver (each file holds two BMPs; the second is the picture) |

The 49 built-in bulbs and the screen saver animations are not here: they are embedded in the program from
`assets/builtin` and `assets/heritage`.

## Provenance and permission

Everything in this folder comes unchanged from the Holiday Lights 5.4 distribution (Tiger Technologies, 2003) and its
add-on bulb collection. It is included in Holiday Lights 6 **with permission**: the project owner holds the rights to
distribute this content with Holiday Lights.

The content is **not** covered by the MIT License of the source code (see `LICENSE`). The bulb art, the music
arrangements and the pictures remain the copyright of their respective authors. All artwork and music are copyrighted by their authors and may not be used for other purposes without their permission.

Every bulb file keeps its author and copyright text exactly as its artist wrote it; Holiday Lights shows it in
**Bulb Credits** (right-click a bulb in the Bulb Factory). The About window and the Help topics "Art Copyright
Information" and "Music Copyright Information" list the same credits as below.
